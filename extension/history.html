Browsing history

Posts from the last 24 hours and their saved answers stay on this device, including after browser restart. Repeated posts are merged.

Turning this off stops new records and keeps your existing history. Viewing saved answers does not make API requests.

Clear all browsing history?

Saved posts and answers will be deleted from this device. This cannot be undone.
